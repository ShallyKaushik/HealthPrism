# HealthPrism Stress Management & Mental Wellness

## Understanding Stress
- Stress is a natural response to challenges, but chronic stress can negatively impact cardiovascular health and immune function.
- Identifying personal stress triggers is the first step toward effective management.

## Immediate Stress Relief Techniques
- Deep Breathing: Practice 4-7-8 breathing (inhale for 4, hold for 7, exhale for 8) to calm the nervous system.
- Progressive Muscle Relaxation: Tense and then release different muscle groups to reduce physical tension.
- Grounding: Use the 5-4-3-2-1 technique (5 things you see, 4 you feel, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste) to stay present.

## Long-term Mental Wellness
- Regular Mindfulness Meditation: Spend 10-15 minutes daily focusing on the present moment without judgment.
- Physical Activity: Exercise releases endorphins, which are natural mood elevators and stress reducers.
- Social Connection: Talking with friends, family, or professionals provides emotional support and perspective.
- Quality Sleep: Prioritize sleep to improve emotional regulation and cognitive resilience against stressors.

## Work-Life Balance
- Set clear boundaries between work and personal time.
- Take regular breaks during the day to recharge and prevent burnout.
- Practice the "Pomodoro Technique" to maintain focus and reduce time-related stress.
