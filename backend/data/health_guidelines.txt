# HealthPrism General Health Guidelines

## Heart Health
- Maintain a balanced diet low in saturated fats and high in fiber.
- Regular cardiovascular exercise (at least 150 minutes of moderate intensity per week) is essential for heart health.
- Monitor blood pressure and cholesterol levels regularly.
- Avoid smoking and limit alcohol consumption to reduce the risk of heart disease.
- Manage stress through mindfulness, meditation, or yoga.

## Nutrition & Diet
- Aim for a variety of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins in your daily meals.
- Stay hydrated by drinking at least 8-10 glasses of water per day.
- Limit processed foods, sugary drinks, and excessive salt intake.
- Understand portion control to maintain a healthy weight and prevent chronic diseases.
- For vegetarians, ensure adequate protein intake through lentils, chickpeas, paneer, and soy products.

## Physical Activity
- Combining aerobic exercises with strength training provides the most comprehensive health benefits.
- Regular movement helps regulate blood sugar, improve mood, and enhance sleep quality.
- Consistency is more important than intensity when starting a new exercise routine.

## Sleep Hygiene
- Aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep per night to allow for physical and cognitive recovery.
- Establish a consistent bedtime routine and limit blue light exposure before sleep.
